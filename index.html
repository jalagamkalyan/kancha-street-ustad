How to Play Kancha

Traditional Indian Street Marbles — Arcade Rules

1
Slide Along the Crease: Use the slider bar (or tap the bottom line) to line up your angle before shooting.
2
Slingshot & Shatter: Drag the big golden Striker backward and release! Knocking marbles outside the chalk circle scores points and gives you a Bonus Turn.
3
Avoid the Ring Trap (Foul): Never let your Striker stop inside the chalk circle (-1 penalty)! Pull the Spin Pad dot down for Backspin so your striker brakes outside the ring.
